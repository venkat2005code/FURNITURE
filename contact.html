Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to the questions we hear most from new clients.

How long does a typical restoration take?
Most projects take between 7 and 21 business days, depending on complexity. Minor touch-ups can be turned around in 1–3 days. We'll confirm a guaranteed delivery date in your quote.
Do you collect from outside London?
Greater London collection is free. For areas outside London, we can arrange insured courier collection at a flat rate of £45–£85 depending on distance. You're also welcome to bring the piece to our Shoreditch workshop.
Is my furniture insured while in your workshop?
Yes. All pieces in our care are covered by our comprehensive workshop insurance up to £50,000 per item. For higher-value antiques, we can arrange additional cover by prior agreement.
Can I supply my own fabric or materials?
Absolutely! We welcome client-supplied fabrics and materials. We'll provide a cutting guide before you purchase to ensure you order the correct quantity and grain direction.
What payment methods do you accept?
We accept bank transfer, all major credit/debit cards, and PayPal. For projects over £500, we offer a 50/50 split payment — half on approval, half on completion.
What if I'm not happy with the result?
We stand behind our work. If you're not completely satisfied, we'll re-do the work at no charge — no questions asked. All completed restorations carry a 12-month workmanship guarantee.
