Our Materials

Authenticity in Every Detail

We use only historically appropriate materials, sourced from specialist suppliers worldwide.

Wood Finishes & Polishes
We stock shellac flake in 20 grades for French polishing, traditional wax blends (beeswax, carnauba, liming), hand-rubbed oils (linseed, tung, Danish), and period lacquers. Every finish is matched to the original by spectrographic analysis where required.
Upholstery Fabrics & Leather
Our fabric library contains 200+ period textiles including period wool, silk velvet, horsehair, brocade, and cotton damask. We hold accounts with leading leather tanneries and can source pigmented, aniline, and semi-aniline hides in 60 standard colours with custom dye available.
Veneers & Marquetry
We hold stock of 40 species of sawn veneer from 0.6mm to 3mm thickness, including rare burr walnut, satinwood, tulipwood, and kingwood for marquetry inlay. Historical hide glue is used exclusively for antique work to ensure reversibility.
Hardware & Brass Fittings
Period-cast brass handles, escutcheons, castors, and hinges sourced from heritage hardware specialists. We can also restore original hardware through chemical and mechanical cleaning processes.
